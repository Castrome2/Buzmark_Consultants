Client & Privacy Policy

1. Engagements

Every engagement begins with a request and a booked consultation. Scope, timelines and deliverables are confirmed in that meeting before production begins.

2. Pricing and payment

  • Training, Consulting and Branding packages are priced up front.
  • All other services are quoted during the consultation meeting.
  • A deposit is payable before delivery begins; the balance is due on handover.
  • Payments are made via M-Pesa, bank transfer or card, and a transaction reference is required.

3. Delivery and revisions

Work moves through the stages shown in your dashboard timeline. Each stage must be completed before the next begins. Reasonable revisions are included within the agreed scope.

4. Cancellations

Requests with no payment can be cancelled by you at any time from the dashboard. Once a deposit is paid and production has started, deposits cover work already performed.

5. Data protection

  • We collect only the details needed to deliver your project and manage your account.
  • Your data is stored securely and is visible only to you and authorised Buzmark staff.
  • We never sell client data to third parties.
  • You may request correction or deletion of your personal information at any time.

6. Confidentiality

Materials, strategies and information shared during an engagement are treated as confidential by both parties unless agreed otherwise in writing.

7. Account security

Sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity. Keep your login credentials private and notify us immediately of any unauthorised access.
